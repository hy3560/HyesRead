HyesSerifSC-SemiBold.ttf is a static and subsetted derivative of
Noto Serif SC (Google Noto fonts), from the locally installed
C:/Windows/Fonts/NotoSerifSC-VF.ttf.

Original font and this derivative are licensed under SIL Open Font License 1.1.
License text and source: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/
Project: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk
The font is generated by tools/build_font_subset.py, not authored manually.
The Android platform's serif family supplies missing ideographs.
